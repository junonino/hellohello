Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding timelines, search engine rankings, ad budgets, and how our systems protect your brand.

Search engine optimization is a compounding asset rather than an overnight switch. For established websites, on-page optimization, directory sync, and technical fixes typically show measurable ranking movement within 60 to 90 days. For newer domain names, Google often takes 3 to 6 months to establish domain authority and trust. Because search engines frequently adjust ranking algorithms and natural backlink profiles fluctuate, our team conducts daily technical site audits and continually generates high-authority backlinks to secure long-term prominence.
When paid campaigns launch on Google, Microsoft (Bing), or Meta, the bidding algorithms enter a 7 to 14-day learning phase. During this time, our systems establish optimal bid pacing so your budget delivers consistent clicks throughout the entire month. If any unspent budget remains from Month 1's ramp-up period, those funds roll directly into Month 2 to maximize impressions and clicks. If a campaign ever delivers under 90% of its budget, our system triggers an automated human review to adjust network distribution and optimize keywords.
Our automated reputation system utilizes an intelligent 2-step feedback process. When customers are prompted for feedback via SMS or email, satisfied clients are seamlessly guided to leave a public 5-star review on Google, Facebook, or Yelp. However, if a customer indicates a neutral or negative experience, their private comments are routed directly to your management team with a 72-hour buffer. This gives you a crucial window to resolve the customer's concerns before any public review request is ever triggered.
We deploy textable Remote Call Forwarding (RCF) tracking numbers across your digital touchpoints. Every inbound call, text message, and website form submission is logged directly inside your unified client dashboard. You have complete transparency: you can view exact lead sources, listen to call audio recordings for staff quality training, and track your true Cost-Per-Lead (CPL) and Click-to-Call conversion rates in real time.
