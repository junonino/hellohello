Our promises

What you can count on, and what we won't do

Marketing has a reputation for big claims. Here is how we work, in plain terms.

We promise

  • You own it: Your domain, website, Google profiles, analytics, and customer data stay in your name. We work as managers and hand everything over if you leave.
  • No long contracts: After setup, service is month-to-month with [30] days' notice to cancel.
  • Honest reporting: A plain-language report every month, including what did not work.

We don't

  • Guarantee rankings: No one can honestly promise first place or a set number of calls.
  • Gate or hide reviews: Every customer gets the same review request.
