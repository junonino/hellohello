Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who We Are

hllohllo marketing ("we," "our," or "us") provides straightforward digital marketing, local search profile management, website maintenance, and review consulting for independent local businesses. If you have questions about this policy or your personal information, contact us at hello@hllohllo.com.

2. What We Collect and Why

When you request a free local audit on our website, we ask for:

  • Your name: So we know who we are communicating with.
  • Business name: To locate and evaluate your public business presence.
  • Website address: To review your site's mobile experience, speed, and call-to-action layout.
  • Business category and primary goal: To ensure our audit reflects relevant local competitors and your immediate priorities.
  • Email address: To send you the written audit and follow up on your questions.
  • Phone number (optional): To contact you by phone if you prefer a call.
  • Message (optional): Any specific questions, challenges, or notes you choose to share.

We collect this information solely to evaluate your business's online visibility, prepare your requested audit, and communicate with you about our services.

3. Who Handles Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with necessary service providers who assist us in operating this site and delivering your audit:

  • Form Processing: Form submissions are handled by our third-party form provider, which processes and securely delivers your message to our inbox.
  • Website Hosting: This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic web request telemetry (such as IP addresses and browser headers) for server operations and security.
  • Fonts: Typography files load from Google Fonts servers, which process basic HTTP requests to render typefaces on your device.

4. Data Retention

We keep your inquiry and audit details only as long as necessary to prepare your audit, follow up with you, and maintain basic business records. If we do not work together, we periodically delete prospective inquiry records.

5. Your Rights: Review and Deletion

You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you or request that we delete your information from our active records. To make a request, send an email to hello@hllohllo.com, and we will fulfill your request promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to how we handle information, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
